# Privacy Intelligence™

## Mission

Privacy Intelligence™ audits websites with **verified probes** — HTML, links, scripts, and Set-Cookie headers — to produce Privacy Score™, findings with Fix Paths™, and educational compliance checklists.

It does **not** invent cookies, fake scores, or legal certifications.

## Surfaces

1. **Self Optimization™** — Privacy Score™ in Overall Growth rollup; detail at `/dashboard/self-optimization/privacy`
2. **Customer reports** — `reports.privacyReport` + Trust · Privacy opportunities (`detectionSource: privacy_engine`)
3. **Privacy Center™** — consent, inventory, export (`/dashboard/settings/privacy`)
4. **AI Privacy Advisor™** — Growth Concierge context + navigation to Privacy Center

## Polarity

| Metric | Higher means |
| --- | --- |
| **Privacy Score™** | Healthier privacy posture |
| **MoneyGap Score™** | More uncaptured opportunity |
