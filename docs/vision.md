# MoneyGap AI Vision

## Mission

Help every business discover the revenue they are missing — and know exactly how to capture it.

## What MoneyGap AI is

MoneyGap AI is an **AI Business Growth Operating System**.

It analyzes a business’s public web presence, detects growth opportunities that are missing, estimates impact, produces an actionable roadmap, and becomes the daily place founders prioritize and execute growth work.

## What MoneyGap AI is not

- Not an SEO tool
- Not another website auditor
- Not a checklist generator
- Not a prompt toy

Technical signals (SEO, content, trust, conversion) matter only when connected to business outcomes.

## Growth chain

Every recommendation must follow this chain:

**Visibility → Traffic → Leads → Customers → Revenue → Growth**

If a finding cannot connect to that chain in plain English, it does not belong in a MoneyGap report.

## North-star question

> What is preventing this business from reaching its full growth potential, and how do we fix it?

## Success criteria

When a founder finishes a report, they should immediately know:

- What is missing
- Why it matters
- What to fix first
- How to fix it (**Fix Path Chooser™** — pick Action Center, code/AI, automation, Hub, or Advisor)
- How to get strategic guidance across the whole business (**Growth Copilot™**)
- Which opportunities could have the greatest impact
- How to build and track implementation (Action Center™ / Advisor)
- How their score and gaps evolve over time (Monitor™)
- **Where they may be heading next** (Predictive Intelligence™ — forecasts, What-If, alerts)
- **Why they should trust each recommendation** (evidence, confidence, detection source)
- **How industry best practices shape priorities** (Knowledge Graph™)

When they open the dashboard each morning, they should know:

- **What to work on today** (Top 3 + Execution Mode)
- How their opportunity portfolio is progressing
- Which goals today’s work supports
- **What risks or opportunities are ahead** (Predictive Center™)

When an agency uses MoneyGap, they should be able to manage clients, brand reports, and track portfolio growth as AI-powered growth consultants.

When a team collaborates in MoneyGap, they should invite staff and clients into one organization workspace, assign and discuss opportunities, run growth sprints, and leave an auditable trail—without splitting into separate client tenants (yet).

When a partner integrates via API, they should be able to submit sites, retrieve scores and opportunities, and subscribe to growth events—MoneyGap as an intelligence layer, not only an app.

When the community uses the Marketplace, they should discover and install growth solutions, industry packs, and Fix Path templates—and see verified patterns as observed insights, not guarantees.

When the platform is launch-ready, operators should see health, checklist progress, and billing configuration—and customers should onboard without friction.

The experience should feel like an experienced business consultant — not a scanner.
