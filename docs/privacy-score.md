# Privacy Score™

## Status bands

| Score | Status |
| --- | --- |
| 90–100 | Excellent |
| 75–89 | Good |
| 50–74 | Needs Attention |
| 0–49 | Critical |

## Trust Engine™ / Growth Score

Privacy Score™ contributes to Self Optimization **Overall Growth** via equal-weight rollup alongside Trust, Crawlability, SEO, and other health scores.

It does **not** rewrite MoneyGap Score™ polarity. High-priority privacy findings may appear as Trust · Privacy opportunities.

## Issue schema

Every finding includes: Problem, Why it matters, Business impact, Evidence, Confidence, Priority, Fix Path™, Estimated time, Verification steps. Estimates labeled **AI Estimate**.

## Unavailable reasons

When Set-Cookie or pages cannot be observed, Privacy Intelligence™ records unavailable reasons instead of inventing findings.
