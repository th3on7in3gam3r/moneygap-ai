# Content Strategy (Growth Academy™)

## Goals

- Improve organic discovery and topical authority
- Educate buyers on Money Gaps, Fix Paths™, and GEO-related themes
- Support product adoption with tutorials and guides
- Maintain editorial quality with human-in-the-loop publishing

## Hub sections

Articles, Guides, Tutorials, Case Studies, Industry Insights, SEO, Conversion Optimization, Technical SEO, AI, Marketing, Product Updates, Release Notes, Success Stories, Research, AI Prompt Library.

## Cadence (recommended)

1. Map buyer-intent themes from recurring Money Gaps
2. Draft with AI assistance when useful
3. Human edit for accuracy, proof, and brand voice
4. Publish with full SEO metadata + internal links
5. Measure views and conversions (AI Estimate baselines where forecasts are shown)

## Research labeling

Research Center pieces must state findings are **aggregated insights**, not guarantees for any single business.
