# Report Framework

## Route

Primary intelligence + MoneyGap report: `/reports/[id]`

Do not change this route. Enhance content within the existing flow.

## Report sections

### 1. Hero

- MoneyGap Score™
- Estimated Annual Revenue Opportunity (AI Estimate)
- Capture potential
- Opportunity summary / next action

### 2. Executive Growth Brief

1–3 paragraphs: what is blocking growth and what to do first.

### 2b. Compared To Your Industry (Phase 13.2)

Additive overview block: industry benchmark, missing capabilities, peer patterns, priority opportunities, classification override. See `docs/industry-intelligence.md`.

### 2c. Industry Growth Playbook (Phase 13)

Ordered Knowledge Graph playbook steps for the classified industry (when available).

### 2d. How You Make Money + Compared To Your Business Model (Phase 13.3)

Revenue architecture stage flow and business-model benchmark gap section. See `docs/business-model-intelligence.md`.

### 2e. Recommended Growth Patterns (Phase 13.4)

Ranked Growth Pattern Library™ recommendations (category, confidence, reasoning) plus Industry Growth Playbook steps linked to pattern slugs when present. See `docs/growth-pattern-library.md`.

### 3. Category score breakdown

Bars for each MoneyGap Engine™ module score.

### 4. Opportunities (primary tab)

Expandable finding cards with full finding model fields + **Action Center™** (Build This For Me, projects, checklists, status).

**Trust Engine™ (Phase 11):** each card shows confidence level, evidence summary, supporting signals, business reasoning, and detection source. See `docs/trust-engine.md`.

### 4b. Trust / decision summary (Phase 11)

Additive overview blocks (same report chrome):

- Top 3 Priorities
- Biggest Opportunity
- Quick Wins / Long-Term Strategy highlights
- Confidence Summary
- Estimated Business Impact
- Next Best Action
- Analysis meta (last analysis date, engine / trust versions)

### 5. Growth Roadmap

Buckets:

- **Today**
- **This Week**
- **This Month**
- **Next Quarter**

Each item: action, expected outcome, difficulty, business impact.

### 6. Business understanding tabs (secondary)

Existing Phase 2 context: Understanding, Audience, Business, Content, Trust.

### 7. Competitive tab (Phase 4)

- Executive Competitive Brief
- Competitor overview (expandable profiles)
- Head-to-head comparison cards (You / Competitor / Gap / Impact / Priority)
- Opportunity, content, authority, monetization gap cards
- Advantages + SWOT
- Top 10 strategic recommendations
- Opportunity timeline
- Soft-fail retry if Competitive Intelligence™ failed

### 8. Advisor tab (Phase 5)

AI Growth Advisor™ chat — report-context answers; optional focus on a specific Money Gap.

### 9. Action tab (Phase 5)

Action Projects™, checklists, progress metrics (gaps closed, opportunity captured, timeline). Lifecycle badges and impact history from Monitor™ (Phase 6).

### 10. Monitor context (Phase 6)

Score evolution, analysis comparisons, and Weekly Growth Briefs feed the dashboard Growth Journey — not a separate report tab. See `docs/moneygap-monitor.md`.

### 11. Agency branding (Phase 7)

When the workspace has agency brand settings, reports show:

- **Prepared by:** Agency / company name
- **Powered by:** MoneyGap AI

Client share links use white-label HTML + print-to-PDF. See `docs/agency-platform.md`.

## AI disclaimer (mandatory near estimates)

> This estimate is generated by AI using the website's visible content, business model, industry, and comparable business patterns. It is intended as strategic guidance and not as a guarantee of financial results.

## Soft-fail

If MoneyGap Engine™ fails after intelligence succeeds, show understanding tabs + retry CTA for the engine.

If Competitive Intelligence™ fails after MoneyGap succeeds, Opportunities and understanding remain usable; Competitive tab shows retry CTA.

Generated assets are **drafts only** — never auto-published. See `docs/growth-advisor.md`.
