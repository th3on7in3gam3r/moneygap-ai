# Integrations

MoneyGap connects analysis and Fix Paths™ to the rest of your growth stack through the Integration Hub. Unavailable connectors **soft-fail** — they never invent findings.

## Why connect / what happens today

Connect tools so MoneyGap can understand your **growth stack beyond the public website** (analytics, CRM, email, CMS, payments, and code).

**Today:**

- Improves Integration Health and the Connection Map for your workspace
- **GitHub** unlocks Developer Mode™ — Project Memory™, IDE prompts, and draft PRs only (never auto-merge to main)
- Soft-fail: Hub issues never block reports; connections do **not** rewrite MoneyGap Score™
- Most connectors stage encrypted credentials; live Engine enrichment from Hub data ships incrementally
- No auto-publish and no auto-email to customers

The first time you click Connect in the Hub, MoneyGap asks you to acknowledge this purpose. After that, Connect proceeds normally.

## How to think about connectors

- Connect what you already use (analytics, publishing, CRM, and related tools as they become available).
- Expect clear status when a connector is off, pending, or erroring.
- Keep human review on anything that could publish or message customers.

## Where to start

1. Read the public [Integrations](/integrations) overview.
2. Open [Integration Hub](/dashboard/integrations) in the dashboard.
3. Read **Why connect**, then Connect a provider (GitHub first if you want Developer Mode™).
4. Treat Hub snapshots as staging — do not assume Engine findings already use every connected app.
