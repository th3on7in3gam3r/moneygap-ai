# MoneyGap Score™

MoneyGap Score™ summarizes how much uncaptured opportunity shows up across your analysis. Lower risk and clearer coverage generally mean a healthier growth posture — always read the score with category context.

## How to read it

- The overall score sits with category breakdowns (for example conversion, SEO, trust, and related modules).
- Category scores explain *where* gaps concentrate — not a single vanity metric.
- Re-scans matter: deltas after Fix Paths™ show whether work moved the needle.

## In the product

1. Open a completed Growth Report from the dashboard.
2. Review the headline MoneyGap Score™ and category tiles.
3. Drill into Money Gaps™ ranked by Opportunity Index™ and estimated impact.
4. Prefer Fix Paths™ that address high-index gaps you can verify.

## Good habits

- Treat score movement as directional evidence, not a guarantee of revenue.
- Pair the score with Crawlability Score™ and Privacy Score™ when those reports are present.
- Use Growth Concierge™ / Copilot for “what should we fix next?” — then confirm in the report.
