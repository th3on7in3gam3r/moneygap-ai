# Browser Extension (Coming Soon)

The MoneyGap AI Chrome extension brings Growth Intelligence to any live page — scan what you’re looking at, see a MoneyGap Score™, and share Fix Path™ links.

**Status:** Coming Soon. It is not available on the Chrome Web Store yet.

## Join the waitlist

[Notify me when it ships →](/extension)

We’ll email you when the extension is ready to install. No install button until a real store listing exists.

## Shared reports (already live)

When the extension publishes a report, recipients can open a public share page such as `/report/ext/[shareId]` with:

- Hostname and overall MoneyGap Score™
- Prioritized Fix Path™ items
- Optional AI Readiness signals
- Links to create a MoneyGap account or join the extension waitlist

## What to expect at launch

- Scan the active tab without opening the full dashboard first
- Share a link teammates and clients can open in a browser
- Upsell into deeper SaaS analysis, monitoring, and Growth Badge™ when you need more

Scores and estimates remain **observed / AI Estimates** — not legal certification or guaranteed ROI.
