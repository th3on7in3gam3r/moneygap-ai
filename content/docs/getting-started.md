# Getting started

MoneyGap AI helps you find revenue your website is leaving on the table — then close the gap with a clear Fix Path™.

## What you need

- A MoneyGap account and workspace
- A public website URL you can analyze
- A few minutes for the first pipeline to finish

## Run your first analysis

1. [Sign up](/sign-up) or [sign in](/sign-in), then open the [dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Add or select a website and start an analysis on a public URL.
3. Wait for the Growth Report to complete — you will see MoneyGap Score™, category scores, and Money Gaps™.
4. Open the highest-priority opportunity and review its Fix Path™.
5. Implement the change with human review, then re-scan to watch deltas over time.

## What “done” looks like for day one

- You can name the top Money Gap™ on your site
- You understand whether the Fix Path™ is code, content, or process
- You know impact figures are **AI Estimates**, not guarantees
