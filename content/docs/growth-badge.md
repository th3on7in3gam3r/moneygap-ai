# Growth Badge™

Display a verified MoneyGap AI Growth Badge™ on your site so visitors can confirm you analyze and improve with MoneyGap.

## Create a badge

1. Open [Growth Badge](/dashboard/badge) in the dashboard.
2. Select a website and a badge style.
3. Click **Create Growth Badge**.
4. Preview the SVG, then **Copy Embed Code**.
5. Open **Verify Badge** to see the public verification page.

## Embed

Paste the HTML into WordPress (Custom HTML / footer), Shopify (theme liquid), Webflow (embed), or any custom site. The image loads from MoneyGap; the link opens `/verify/MG-######`.

## What verification shows

- Verified or revoked status
- Website analyzed (name + domain)
- Analysis date and observed MoneyGap Score™
- Growth Journey (before / after / points) when score history exists

Figures are **AI Estimates** — not a legal certification or guaranteed ROI.
