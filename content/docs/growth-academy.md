# Growth Academy™

Growth Academy™ is MoneyGap’s educational hub — playbooks and articles that map to the same themes as Fix Paths™ (SEO, conversion, trust, crawl, privacy, and more).

## Public Academy

Browse published guides at [/academy](/academy). Articles are written for operators and founders who want depth without replacing live analysis on your own site.

## How Academy and product fit together

1. Run an analysis to find live Money Gaps™.
2. Use Academy articles to learn the pattern behind a gap.
3. Return to the report Fix Path™ to implement on *your* URLs.
4. Re-scan — education plus execution closes the loop.

## Editorial note

Academy content is curated. AI may assist drafts for editors; published articles still go through human review. Impact claims stay labeled as AI Estimates where they appear.
