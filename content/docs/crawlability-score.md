# Crawlability Score™

Crawlability Score™ is a **health** signal: higher generally means crawlers and assistants can reach and understand important pages more reliably. It sits alongside MoneyGap Score™ — it does not replace conversion or revenue framing.

## What it looks at

Typical probes include link structure, reachability, and related crawl hygiene. Exact module coverage evolves with the product; the report always explains findings in context.

## How to use it

1. Open a Growth Report that includes a Crawlability section.
2. Note the overall score and listed findings.
3. Prioritize blockers that hide money pages (pricing, signup, key landing pages).
4. Ship Fix Paths™, then re-scan to confirm the score and findings moved.

## What it is not

- Not a Google ranking guarantee
- Not a substitute for Search Console or your host’s crawl logs
- Not auto-fixed — you still implement and review changes
