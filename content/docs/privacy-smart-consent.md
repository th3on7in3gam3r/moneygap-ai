# Privacy and Smart Consent™

MoneyGap includes Privacy Intelligence™ so visitors and teams can understand what is collected and control optional categories.

## Smart Consent™

On first visit (and when preferences need refresh), Smart Consent™ lets people accept all, reject optional categories, or customize:

- **Essential** — required to run the product (cannot be disabled)
- **Analytics**, **Personalization**, **Product Improvement** — optional where active

Theme (light / dark) works without opening consent. Use **Privacy preferences** in the footer anytime to reopen Smart Consent™.

## Privacy Center™

Signed-in users can open **Settings → Privacy** for inventory, history, export, and FAQ-style guidance about MoneyGap processing.

## Privacy Score™

Customer and self-optimization reports may include Privacy Score™ — a health-oriented view of consent UX, cookie practices, and related signals. Higher is generally healthier; treat findings as Fix Paths™, not legal advice.
