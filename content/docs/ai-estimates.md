# AI Estimates and human review

MoneyGap uses AI to draft guidance, metadata, prompts, and **impact framing**. Revenue and traffic figures labeled as **AI Estimates** are directional — never guarantees of ROI.

## Principles

- **Drafts only** — no auto-publish to CRM, email, production CMS, or main without human review
- **Human in the loop** — confirm Fix Paths™ before you ship
- **Transparent labels** — treat Opportunity Index™ and dollar estimates as AI-assisted signals
- **Soft-fail integrations** — missing connectors never invent findings

## Where you will see AI

- Growth reports and Money Gap™ writeups
- Copilot / Growth Concierge™ recommendations
- IDE prompts and Developer Mode drafts
- Growth Academy™ and Action Center content drafts (where enabled)

## Good practice

1. Read evidence and what’s missing before trusting an estimate.
2. Prefer small, reviewable diffs for code Fix Paths™.
3. Re-scan after shipping so deltas are measured, not assumed.
